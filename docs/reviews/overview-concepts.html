Private by Design. Local by Default.Your Data. Your Device. Your Control.

01 / WHY IT MATTERS

Your life is not a dataset.

Your financial records, conversations, routines, and personal documents are parts of your life. Using an application shouldn't automatically mean turning them into another company's stored asset.

Connected services can be valuable. But collecting more information can increase exposure — sometimes discovered only through a breach notification. Keeping information locally doesn't eliminate security risks; the goal is meaningful choice, not a guarantee.

02 / OUR APPROACH

Useful software. Less unnecessary exposure.

These are our design intentions, not a claim that every product already works this way.

Not everything needs the cloud.

Modern devices can do many useful tasks locally. When information doesn't need to leave your device, design to keep it there.

Intelligence without unnecessary exposure.

AI shouldn't need your entire personal history. Make external assistance optional and user-initiated, with clarity about what is sent and why.

Your information remains yours.

Design for clear storage choices and meaningful control over access, retention, sharing, export, and deletion.

Your information. Your decision.

One philosophy. A family of personal applications.

We envision tools for finances, personal routines, documents, and everyday life built around the same principle: your information remains under your control.
