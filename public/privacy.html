Website privacy notice

Updated October 11, 2026. This notice describes the Amanchi Labs company website, not the behavior of WealthPrivate, DermaPrivate, or other applications.

Visiting this website

The website presents company and product information. It does not offer account registration, payments, or a contact form. The current website source does not include analytics or advertising trackers, or code that stores visitor data in cookies or browser storage.

Serving a page necessarily sends your network request to the hosting and delivery services. These services may process information such as your IP address, request URL, browser details, and timestamps for delivery, security, and operational purposes. Their logging and retention practices are controlled by those services; the website's local-first philosophy is not a promise that no website request information is processed externally.

Fonts and external links

The main site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser makes requests to Google to retrieve the font styles and files. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Links to product websites and other services take you to separate destinations with their own data-handling practices. This company website does not establish the privacy behavior of those products.

Contacting us

Email links open your chosen email application. If you send us a message, your email provider and ours process that message, and Amanchi Labs receives the information you choose to include so we can respond. Please avoid sending sensitive financial documents or personal records in an initial inquiry.

Questions or privacy requests: founder@amanchilabs.com. Product support: support@amanchilabs.com.
